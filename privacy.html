Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Introduction

Folio ("we", "us", or "our") is a book tracking app for households. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use Folio, how we use it, and the choices you have.

By creating an account, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

2. Information we collect

Information you provide directly

  • Account information — your email address and password when you sign up, and the display name you choose.
  • Household information — your household's name, its members, and its invite code.
  • Your household's library — the copies of books your household owns, including format, where each copy lives in your home (e.g. "Bedroom shelf"), notes about the copy (such as who gave it to you), tags and collections. Books you add by hand (title, author and other details you type in) are visible only to your household.
  • Your reading — your own reading status, star ratings, thoughts, and the dates you started and finished a book.
  • Lists and Wishlists — lists of books you create (shared with your household or kept to yourself) and your Wishlist, which your household can see.
  • Recommendations — books you recommend to household members.

Information collected automatically

  • Performance data — for a small sample of sessions, how long screens and requests take to load. This is handled by Sentry (see Section 5).
  • Crash and diagnostic data — if the app crashes or encounters an error, we collect technical information including device model, operating system version, and a stack trace. This is handled by Sentry (see Section 5).
  • Device and connection information — device type, operating system version, and IP address.
  • App version — which version of Folio you last opened, and when. We use this to make sure everyone is on a version that works properly with their library.

Permissions on your device

  • Camera — used only when you tap Scan barcode, to read the barcode on a book. The barcode is read on your device; no photos or video are stored or sent to us.

Camera access is optional, and you can turn it off at any time in your device's Settings → Folio. You can still search for books or add them by hand without it.

Information from third parties

  • Google Books API — when you scan a barcode or search for a book, we fetch metadata including title, author, cover image, publisher, publication year, genre, and ISBN. This data is stored in our database and is not edited after it is saved.

3. How we use your information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Create and manage your account and household
  • Store and display your personal library, reading history, and book data
  • Enable household sharing features — the shared household library, collections, shared lists, Wishlists, and recommendations between members
  • Identify and fix bugs and crashes in the app (via Sentry)
  • Improve the reliability and performance of Folio
  • Make sure you're using a version of Folio that works properly, and let you know when it's time to update
  • Respond to support requests

We do not use your data to serve advertising. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

4. Household data sharing

Folio is designed for shared use within a household. If you are part of a household with other members, please be aware that:

  • Books belong to the household. Everyone in your household can see, edit and organize the household's copies, including their locations, notes, tags and collections.
  • Your reading is visible to your household. Your status, star ratings and thoughts on a book can be seen by the other members (only you can change them).
  • Lists you mark as shared, and your Wishlist, are visible to your household. Lists you keep to yourself are visible only to you.
  • Recommendations are visible to the person who sent them and the person who received them.
  • Joining a household: when you join another household, the books, locations, tags and collections from your old household move with you into the new one.

If you do not wish to share particular information with your household, we recommend not entering it into the app.

5. Third-party services

Folio uses the following third-party services. Each has its own privacy policy.

Supabase — our database and authentication provider. Your account credentials, library data, and all content you enter into Folio is stored on Supabase infrastructure.
Supabase Privacy Policy

Google Books API — used to retrieve book metadata when you scan a barcode or search for a title. Queries include the ISBN or search terms you enter.
Google Privacy Policy

Sentry — we use Sentry for crash reporting, error monitoring, and measuring app performance. If the app crashes, Sentry automatically collects diagnostic information including device model, OS version, app version, and a technical stack trace. Sentry does not collect the content of your library, your notes or your thoughts.
Sentry Privacy Policy

6. Data retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, your profile, your reading (statuses, ratings and thoughts), your lists, your Wishlist and your recommendations are permanently deleted.

Your household's library belongs to the household, so if other members remain, the household's books, locations, tags and collections stay with them. If you are the last member of your household, deleting your account also permanently deletes the household and its whole library.

We occasionally take a backup of the Folio database to protect against data loss. Backups are accessible only to the Folio developer, are used only to restore data, and are kept only as long as needed for that, so deleted data may remain in a backup for a while before it's gone for good.

Crash and performance reports are kept by Sentry for up to 30 days.

7. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Delete your account and personal data
  • Export your data

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the address in Section 11. You can also delete your account directly within the app via Profile → Delete account.

8. If you're in the UK or Europe

If you live in the United Kingdom or the European Economic Area, data protection law (the UK GDPR and the EU GDPR) gives you some extra rights, and asks us to explain a few more things.

Who is responsible for your data. The developer of Folio is the "controller" of your personal data. You can reach us at the address in Section 11.

Why we're allowed to use it.

  • To provide Folio to you — your account, household, library, reading, lists, Wishlist and recommendations are used so the app can do what you signed up for.
  • Our legitimate interests — crash reports, performance data and app version are used to keep Folio working, secure and up to date. You can object to this (see below).

Where your data is stored. Folio's database (Supabase) and crash reporting (Sentry) are based in the United States, so your data is transferred there. These providers protect transferred data with the safeguards approved for this, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses and the UK International Data Transfer Addendum.

Your extra rights. As well as the rights in Section 7, you can ask us to restrict how we use your data, and object to us using it for our legitimate interests. We'll reply within one month. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, you can complain to a data protection authority: in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk); in the EU, the authority in your country.

9. Children's privacy

Folio is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will notify you within the app or by email before the changes take effect. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.
